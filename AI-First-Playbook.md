# AI First — Operating Playbook

**For:** the person running content, community & sales
**Goal:** build a trusted brand that helps people navigate AI → reach €30k/month, then scale toward millions/year.
**Our edge:** not software — **value, honesty, curation, and community.** People trust us. That's the whole business.

---

## 1. Who we serve — three categories

Everyone who lands picks a category. Everything they see is filtered for them.

| | 🧍 **Everyday** | 🛡️ **Future-Proof** | 🏪 **Business** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | Wants AI to make life easier | Doesn't want to lose their job | Small business owner |
| Driver | Curiosity, save time | Fear — stay relevant | ROI — save money, grow |
| Pays? | Low | Medium | **High** |
| Their job for us | Wide free funnel (volume) | Audience + membership | **Revenue engine** |

**Rule:** *Everyday* is a free net to catch people. The money is in **Future-Proof** (membership) and **Business** (services + training). Everyday users convert into those two over time.

**Our promise to all three:** cut the hype, tell the truth, and give *one thing that matters + one real example + one action* — every time.

## 2. The weekly rhythm (do this every week, never miss it)

Consistency beats brilliance. A boring schedule we keep beats an ambitious one we drop. Hold this for 90 days minimum.

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Post community thread: *"What are you working on with AI this week?"* | 15 min |
| **Mon–Fri** | Write + publish **3 LinkedIn posts** (Mon, Wed, Fri) — see templates §4 | 30 min each |
| **Thu** | Send the **Weekly Brief** (newsletter) — see template §3 | 1–2 hrs |
| **Thu** | Turn that brief into a **website guide page** (same content, reused) | 20 min |
| **Fri** | Post community thread: *"Weekly Wins — what did AI do for you this week?"* | 15 min |
| **Ongoing** | Reply to every comment & community post within a day | — |

**The write-once trick:** the Weekly Brief is the core. It becomes → 1 email → 1 website guide → 3 LinkedIn posts. One real piece of work, published everywhere.

## 3. The Weekly Brief — template

Send every Thursday. Keep it short, honest, skimmable. Always this structure:

```
Subject: [The one thing that matters this week]

Hi [name],

👉 THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS
[2–3 sentences: the single most important AI development or idea this week,
and why it matters for a normal person. Cut everything else.]

🏆 A REAL WIN
[One short story of a real person — a member, a business, or a case study —
who used AI to get a result. Name the person/role and the outcome.]

⚡ YOUR 10-MINUTE ACTION
[One small thing the reader can do today. A prompt to try, a tool to test,
a setting to change. Small enough to actually do.]

— [signature]

P.S. [one honest line: something overhyped to ignore, or a link to a
tool/prompt on the site]
```

**Do:** be specific, be honest, name real results.
**Don't:** list 10 tools, hype things, sound like a robot or a guru.

## 4. LinkedIn posts — how + 10 starter ideas (Future-Proof first)

Post Mon/Wed/Fri. First line is everything — it decides if anyone reads. Then value, then a soft nudge to subscribe.

**Structure of every post:**
1. **Hook** (1 line that stops the scroll — a fear, a bold claim, a number)
2. **Value** (3–6 short lines — one useful, specific idea)
3. **Nudge** (1 line: "I break these down every week in the AI First brief — link in profile")

**10 starter hooks for "Future-Proof" (people worried about their jobs):**
1. "The people losing jobs to AI aren't losing them to AI. They're losing them to coworkers who use AI."
2. "5 tasks in your job that AI can already do — and how to become the person who runs it instead."
3. "If your boss could replace you with ChatGPT, do this before they figure it out."
4. "I asked 10 managers what makes an employee 'AI-safe.' Same answer every time."
5. "The 1 AI skill that quietly makes you the most valuable person on your team."
6. "You don't need to learn to code. You need to learn to delegate to AI. Here's the difference."
7. "3 signs your job is at risk from AI — and the 30-minute fix for each."
8. "Everyone's panicking about AI. The calm ones are just doing this one thing."
9. "How a [accountant/nurse/teacher] used AI to get promoted instead of replaced."
10. "The boring truth about keeping your job in the AI age (no one's selling this because it's free)."

Rotate the same ideas for Everyday and Business categories with their own angle.

## 5. Community rituals — run these

Two threads a week, forever. They only work if run consistently long enough to become expected.

- **Monday — "What are you working on?"** → members share their goal this week.
- **Friday — "Weekly Wins"** → members post what AI did for them. Celebrate every one.
- **Monthly — Member Spotlight** → interview one member, share their story. This creates aspiration and belonging.

Golden rule: **reply to everyone, fast.** Early on, our energy *is* the community.

## 6. Making money — what to sell

### A) Business offer (fastest cash — start here)
Sell to small businesses. This funds everything.

> **"AI Setup + Management: €3,000 to install AI in your business so it saves 10+ hours/month, then €750/month to run and improve it."**

**Pitch message (LinkedIn DM / email):**
```
Hi [name],

I help small businesses use AI to save 10+ hours a month on
[admin / marketing / customer replies].

I recently helped [example business] do exactly that — [result].

Would it be useful if I showed you the 3 places AI could save your
team the most time? No cost, 20 minutes.

— [name]
```
**Target:** contact 20 businesses/week. Close 3–5/month. Retainers **stack** — keep old clients while adding new → build toward €30k/month.

### B) Corporate training (high-ticket value, no software)
Businesses pay **€5,000–20,000** to train their team on AI. Pure value delivery. Pitch companies once we have case studies.

### C) Membership (recurring — the path to millions)
€/month for individuals (mainly Future-Proof): full prompt library, personalized tool picks, community, deeper playbooks.
- **Free:** weekly brief, basic tool search, sample prompts.
- **Paid (~€20–30/mo):** the rest.

### D) Passive
- **Affiliate:** AI tools pay recurring commissions when our users sign up via the tool search.
- **Sponsored placements:** once traffic is big, AI companies pay to be featured.

